Project Summary

Wanjiku, a fresh-produce vendor at Gikomba Market, needs to understand her stall's sales performance, determine whether her business is growing, identify products requiring restocking, and establish where to focus her business efforts. This project uses 14 days of daily sales records and inventory data to assess overall revenue, weekly growth, daily sales performance, weekday versus weekend trends, stock levels, and product gross margins. The analysis provides practical, data-driven recommendations to help Wanjiku make better restocking decisions, prepare for periods of higher demand, and improve her business performance.

How to Run

Open the notebook in Google Colab: "Open stall_analysis.ipynb in Google Colab" (https://colab.research.google.com/github/mark-ochieng/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

Alternatively, download the notebook and run it using Jupyter Notebook.

Top Three Findings

1. Overall Sales: Wanjiku generated total revenue of KES 46,450 over the 14-day period, with average daily sales of KES 3,317.86.

2. Business Growth: Week 2 sales increased by 10.66%, rising from KES 22,050 in Week 1 to KES 24,400 in Week 2.

3. Weekend Performance: Average weekend sales were KES 4,900, compared with KES 2,685 on weekdays. Weekend sales were therefore 82.50% higher.

Key Business Recommendations

- Prioritize restocking tomatoes and Sukuma because their current stock quantities are below their respective reorder levels.
- Prepare for higher weekend demand by ensuring sufficient stock availability, particularly before Saturdays and Sundays.
- Monitor avocado sales because it recorded the highest gross margin at 42.86%.
- Continue monitoring weekly sales to determine whether the positive growth trend is sustainable.
