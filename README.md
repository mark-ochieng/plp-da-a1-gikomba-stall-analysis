Project Overview

This project analyzes 14 days of sales and stock records for Wanjiku, a fresh-produce stall owner at Gikomba Market. The objective is to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, compare weekday and weekend performance, assess stock levels, and calculate product gross margins. Using core Python, the analysis provides actionable insights to help Wanjiku make informed decisions about restocking, inventory management, and business growth.

How to Run

Open the notebook in Google Colab: "Open in Colab" (https://colab.research.google.com/github/mark-ochieng/plp-da-a1-gikomba-stall-analysis/blob/main/stall_analysis.ipynb)

Top Three Findings

1. Business growth: Week 2 generated KES 24,400 compared to KES 22,050 in Week 1, representing a 10.66% increase in sales.

2. Weekend performance: Average weekend sales were KES 4,900 compared to weekday average sales of KES 2,685, approximately 82.50% higher.

3. Stock management: Tomatoes (18 kg against a 25 kg reorder level) and Sukuma (9 kg against a 15 kg reorder level) require restocking attention.
